# Calendar
A Calendar with a unique design and approximately 1.5 years of battery life

### Update
The Build is finished and i have multiple prototypes working. Still I decided not to post instructions on building one as the whole thing needs a redesign first. The ESP32 is crashing as when breaking, the motors induce a current causing too big voltage fluctuations, which crashes my microcontroller. It is an old project of mine and i would do things rather differently by now, which is why i want to  the redesign the whole thing. It should then be cheaper simpler and more stable, as I plan on using cheap geared brushed motors together with hall-efect sensors, also eliminating the need for mechanical end stops. Sadly currently im just not having the time for it and i hope that i can come back to it one day.
